Practical AI implementation, grounded in your business

AiBusinessCopilot helps established teams improve the work they already do. We identify where AI can reduce manual effort, choose an appropriate approach, and build around the people and systems involved.

Who you work with

AiBusinessCopilot is run by Ty, based in Scottsdale, Arizona. He works hands-on with businesses to understand their workflows and turn useful AI capabilities into practical implementations.

We use AI in our own work every day, from research and planning to software development and preparing business materials. That experience informs how we evaluate tools, test results, and identify where human review matters.

How we approach the work

We start with a specific workflow and a result worth improving. Your existing tools, information, permissions, and team shape the solution.

Success measures are agreed before implementation. The aim is to establish what improves, what it costs to run, and whether the result justifies further investment.
