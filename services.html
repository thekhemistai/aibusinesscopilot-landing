AI implementation around your existing tools

We scope each system around a defined workflow, the information it needs, and the person responsible for reviewing its output. Common starting points include:

  • ChatGPT setup for teams: shared instructions, approved reference material, and a repeatable way to draft and review work.
  • Reporting and analysis: gather approved information, summarize recurring reports, and flag gaps for a person to check.
  • Documents and handoffs: prepare drafts, check requests for missing information, and route work with clear approval steps.
  • Internal knowledge: help employees find answers in the procedures, policies, and records they already use.
  • AI workflow integrations: connect the agreed steps to existing systems when access, data quality, and technical fit support it.

Team onboarding and ongoing support

We choose the approach that fits the workflow: a ChatGPT setup, an API integration, an agent, or a combination. The scope defines the connections, testing, team onboarding, and handover. Your team receives instructions for using the system, reviewing its output, and handling exceptions.

Implementation is quoted after assessment. Ongoing support is optional and scoped separately, with its coverage and responsibilities stated in writing.
