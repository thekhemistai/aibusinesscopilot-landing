Common questions

The questions everyone asks first.

Will it send emails or do anything without my OK?+

No. The assistant prepares work — drafts, schedules, summaries, quotes — and queues it up for you. Sending, posting, and paying stay human actions. We set that up as a hard rule, not a suggestion.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?+

Sometimes it will — which is exactly why everything arrives as a draft for your review instead of an action that already happened. When something is off, we tighten the rules and context so it doesn't repeat. You're never cleaning up after a message that already went out.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. We handle the setup, the connections, and the rules. You message your assistant in your own words — if you can describe what you want done, you can run it.

Does it only work in English?+

No. The AI tools we set up work in many languages, Spanish included. A customer can write in whatever language they use and get a clean draft reply in the same language, and you can message your assistant in whichever language you think in.

Whose AI account is it? Do I own the setup?+

Yours. The AI account is in your name, on your billing, with your data. The playbooks and configuration we build are yours to keep — if we ever part ways, everything keeps working.

What about my customer data?+

It stays in your accounts and your tools. We connect the assistant narrowly — only the systems the job actually needs — and we work with established providers. The simple details are in our Privacy Policy and AI Risk Disclosure.

How long does setup take?+

A single fix is typically up and running within one to two weeks, with a playbook and setup support included. Bigger foundations take longer — and we scope everything before you commit to anything.

What does it cost?+

The public starting point is the $999 AI Business Audit. We review where AI can save time, recover missed revenue, or reduce bottlenecks, then quote the actual buildout after the audit. If you hire us to build, the audit fee can credit toward the buildout.
