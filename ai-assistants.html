Where ChatGPT, integrations, and agents fit

A useful AI implementation starts with a piece of work: who does it, what information it needs, what happens next, and how the result will be checked.

The approach depends on those answers. These options can work together.

ChatGPT: support for work your team directs

ChatGPT can help people analyze information, prepare documents, research questions, and develop useful first drafts. Relevant business context and examples help shape the work.

This can be a useful starting point when employees already direct the task and review the result. Available tools and connections depend on the product, account, permissions, and setup.

API integrations: AI within an existing process

An API lets software exchange requests and results. An AI integration can place a model's capabilities inside a workflow your business already uses.

For example, a request could be checked for missing information, categorized, and prepared for the appropriate team to review. The integration needs defined inputs, tested outputs, permissions, and failure handling.

Agents: coordination across multiple steps

An agent uses AI to decide which permitted steps or tools to use while working toward a defined goal.

That may help when a task involves gathering context, evaluating what to do next, and coordinating work across systems. A predictable sequence may be better served by straightforward automation.

A practical example

Consider customer onboarding. Sales collects information, operations checks readiness, and an account manager prepares the next steps.

AI might help check the intake, summarize relevant records, and prepare a handoff. The team still defines acceptable output, review points, and what happens when something is missing or wrong.

Choose by fit, then measure

Assess the current effort, volume, quality, and costs. Start with a bounded workflow and compare the completed results, including human review and exceptions.

The right choice is the one that improves the workflow enough to justify its complexity and running cost.
