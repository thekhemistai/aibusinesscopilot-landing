Make ChatGPT useful in the work your team repeats

For a small business, a useful AI project starts with a recurring job, the tools already in use, and a clear way to tell whether the work improves.

You can begin with one workflow and expand when the results justify it.

Look for work with a clear starting point

Recurring reports

Preparing a weekly report may involve collecting figures, checking notes, and explaining changes. AI can help prepare the analysis and narrative from approved inputs for someone to verify.

Customer handoffs

New work often arrives with incomplete details. A workflow can help organize the request, identify missing information, and prepare the next step for review.

Internal procedures

Employees repeatedly searching for the same instructions may benefit from a way to find and summarize answers from approved business documents.

Give it the information and boundaries it needs

Start with relevant source material, examples of acceptable work, and rules for review. Check what the chosen account and tools support before connecting business information.

Use only information approved for that workflow. Decide who checks the result and how errors return to a person.

Check whether it earns its place

Record how long the work takes now, how often it happens, and what a correct result looks like. Compare that with the new process, including review, corrections, and software costs.

Hours freed up are useful capacity. Actual spending reduced needs separate evidence. A faster draft only helps if the finished work meets the required standard.
